## Questions

### 1. "What's your social impact?"
💡 Penalties avoided and time saved → money stays in local businesses.
🗣 "Small business owners stop losing money to penalties and uncollected tax credits, and they get their nights back. That money stays in the business for wages and growth. That's SDG 8. And it's built by Filipinos for Filipino businesses, starting in Region 2. That's SDG 9."

### 2. "How will you measure impact?"
💡 Numbers the owner can feel.
🗣 "In our pilots we'll measure three things: hours saved on paperwork per month, pesos of 2307 credits recovered, and late filings avoided."
⛔ Don't claim results you haven't measured yet. Say "we will measure."

### 3. "How does this help the government?"
💡 Easier filing → better compliance → more correct tax collection.
🗣 "When filing is easier and records are complete, more small businesses file correctly and on time. That directly supports the Ease of Paying Taxes Act and the move to e-invoicing."

### 4. "Why Region 2 and not Manila?"
💡 It's home, it's underserved, and it proves the product anywhere.
🗣 "We're from here, our incubator is NVSU Sabatan, and tech companies mostly focus on Metro Manila. If it works for a law office in Nueva Vizcaya, it works anywhere in the country."

### 5. "Can a sari-sari store use this?"
💡 Yes eventually; professionals first.
🗣 "It's on our roadmap, but not first. We start with professionals who have the most paperwork. The same core can power a simpler system for micro-merchants once we've proven it."

### 6. "What about owners who aren't good with technology?"
💡 That's why it's a chat assistant in Taglish.
🗣 "That's exactly why Juan is something you talk to. Owners can type in Taglish instead of learning menus, and during pilots we onboard them in person."

### 7. "Won't AI take bookkeepers' jobs?"
💡 These owners can't afford a bookkeeper; accountants get better work.
🗣 "Most of these businesses can't afford a bookkeeper now; the owner does it alone at night. Juan takes that load. And records come out clean and organized for their accountant, so accountants spend time advising instead of re-encoding."

### 8. "Are there risks to users?"
💡 Wrong numbers and data privacy, both handled.
🗣 "Two: wrong tax numbers and data privacy. For the first, the AI never computes taxes, a CPA certifies the rules, and the owner approves every return. For the second, each business's data is isolated in the database, and we register with the National Privacy Commission."

### 9. "Who benefits besides the owner?"
💡 Their clients, their staff, their accountant.
🗣 "Their clients, who sign 2307s online instead of chasing paper. Their staff, who see their tasks by role. And their accountant, who gets clean records."

## Real example (Clarity is scored on real-world examples)
> "One law office we interviewed bills corporate clients who withhold tax. If the client never sends the 2307, the lawyer loses that credit and effectively pays the tax twice. Multiply that by every client, every quarter. That's money a small office can't afford to lose."
