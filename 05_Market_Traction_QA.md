> **Owner:** Member C.
> Judges score "market potential" and "implementation": who buys, how you reach them, and whether the plan is realistic.

## Understand it first

**Your market is a funnel, not one big number.** Judges distrust "1.24 million MSMEs" on its own. Show you know where you start:

| Stage | Who | Size | Why them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Now** | Region 2 businesses via NVSU Sabatan | **100 paying** in 12 months | Close to us, underserved, our incubator connects us |
| **2. Next** | Lawyers, contractors, real estate nationwide | **120,000+** | Most paperwork, 2307s every month, can afford ₱299 |
| **3. Later** | All PH MSMEs | **1.24 million** (DTI 2024) | Same core, simpler packs |

📌 101,368 lawyers (Supreme Court list) · 19,168 PCAB-licensed contractors · 5,294 real estate establishments (PSA).

**Who is the "best fit" customer?** Any business that **invoices other businesses and gets tax withheld.** That's exactly the paperwork JuanStack removes. Say this sentence; it shows you know your customer precisely.

**Traction, honestly:** a working product (juanstack.com), interviews with 4 businesses, an incubator. **No paying users yet**, on purpose. That's normal at this stage and fine to say confidently.

## Questions

### 1. "Who is your first customer?"
💡 Small law offices and contractors in Region 2.
🗣 "Small law offices and contractors here in Region 2, like the ones we interviewed. They bill other businesses, get tax withheld, and have no bookkeeper, so they have the most paperwork and the most to gain."

### 2. "Why lawyers first?"
💡 Most 2307s, enough of them, can pay, and we've talked to them.
🗣 "Four reasons. They bill companies, so they deal with 2307s constantly. There are over 100,000 of them. ₱299 is easy for them. And we already interviewed three law offices that confirmed the problem."

### 3. "Your market is 1.24 million. Isn't that unrealistic?"
💡 That's the ceiling, not the plan.
🗣 "That's the total, not our target. Our goal for 12 months is 100 paying businesses in Region 2. Then the 120,000 professionals. The 1.24 million shows the room to grow."
↪️ *"Not all 101,000 lawyers are in practice."* "True. Even if only one in ten is in private practice, that's 10,000, and we need 100 this year."

### 4. "How will you get your first 100 users?"
💡 Local, personal, cheap first; ads only grow with revenue.
🗣 "Region 2 first. NVSU Sabatan connects us to local businesses, and we go to DTI Negosyo Centers, chambers of commerce and professional groups with live demos. Every user who refers someone gets a free month. That's about 9 new paying users a month, which is realistic for a local team."

### 5. "What traction do you have?"
💡 Built product + validated problem + incubator. Honest about no revenue.
🗣 "Three things. A working product live at juanstack.com. Interviews with three law offices and a construction firm that confirmed the problem. And NVSU Sabatan helping us recruit pilot users. Pilots with the businesses we interviewed are first once we're funded."
📌 Barawid Law Office · office of Atty. Armi Liza M. Mateo-Lazaro · Baguidudol Law Office · Lacuata Constructions.

### 6. "Do you have paying customers?"
💡 Not yet, and that's a responsible choice.
🗣 "Not yet, and that's deliberate. We don't want to charge anyone for tax work until a licensed CPA certifies our rules. That's the first thing the funding pays for, then paid plans launch in months 4 to 6."
⛔ Never stretch: no "we have users" if they're not real users.

### 7. "What did the interviews tell you?"
💡 The problem isn't bad management; it's that no one tracks the back office.
🗣 "That owners don't lose money because they manage badly. They lose it because nobody tracks the back office: 2307s never collected, late filings, records scattered across chat apps and paper."

### 8. "Why now?"
💡 The law is forcing small businesses to go digital, and they're not ready.
🗣 "The Ease of Paying Taxes Act changed invoicing and withholding rules, and e-invoicing becomes required for covered taxpayers by end of 2026. Small businesses have to go digital now, and most have no staff or tools to do it."
📌 RA 11976 · BIR RR 26-2025 (e-invoicing transition) · digital payments now 57.4% of PH retail payment volume (BSP 2025).

### 9. "What's your 12-month plan?"
💡 Make it safe → launch → grow.
🗣 "Months 1 to 3: make it safe. Security testing, CPA review, business and privacy registration, and pilots. Months 4 to 6: launch paid plans and the mobile apps. Months 7 to 12: grow across Region 2 and professional groups to 100 paying businesses."

### 10. "What are your biggest risks?"
💡 Name them before they do, with the fix for each.
🗣 "Three. Tax accuracy, so a CPA certifies our rules and the owner approves every return. Data trust, so we do security testing and register with the National Privacy Commission. And adoption by busy owners, so we start with personal onboarding and pilots, not ads."

### 11. "How do you onboard owners who aren't techy?"
💡 Guided setup + human help + talk instead of click.
🗣 "Setup is a guided process under two minutes. Pilot users get hands-on onboarding from us, including moving their old records in. After that, they can just ask Juan in Taglish."

### 12. "What happens when BIR rules change?"
💡 One engine, update once, everyone gets it.
🗣 "All tax rules live in one shared engine. We update it once, every business gets the change, and a CPA reviews new BIR regulations every quarter."

### 13. "What about the mobile app?"
💡 Web works on phones today; native apps are coming.
🗣 "The web app already works on phones. iOS and Android apps are in development in React Native, and publishing them is part of the funding."

### 14. "How will you measure success?"
💡 Business numbers + impact numbers.
🗣 "Business: 100 paying businesses and break-even at 15. Impact: in the pilots we measure hours saved per month and pesos of 2307 credits recovered."
