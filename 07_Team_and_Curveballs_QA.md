# Team & Curveball Q&A

> **Owner:** Member C for team questions; whoever is strongest for curveballs.
> Curveballs test **composure**. The answer matters less than staying calm and honest.

---

## Team

### 1. "Who's on your team, and why are you the right team?"
💡 Each person owns a part, and you've already built it.
🗣 **[FILL IN: 15 seconds total. Name, course and year, what they own.]**
> Example shape: "I'm ___, I lead the product and built the system. ___ handles the business model and finances. ___ leads research, customers and pitching. We're NVSU students, and we built a working product, not just slides, with NVSU Sabatan backing us."

### 2. "What happens after you graduate?"
💡 This is the plan, not a school project.
🗣 **[FILL IN honestly.]** Suggested: "JuanStack is our plan after graduation. We register it as a business at 30 paying users, and break-even is only 15, so it can support itself early."

### 3. "Are you full-time on this?"
🗣 "We're students, so not full-time yet. But the product is built and the next 12 months are planned. The grant lets us focus on it."

### 4. "You're not accountants. Who handles the tax side?"
💡 Admit it, then show the safeguard.
🗣 "Right, and we don't pretend to be. Our tax engine follows published BIR regulations, and a licensed CPA certifies every computation before we charge anyone, plus quarterly reviews of new rules. It's in our budget."

### 5. "Who are your mentors?"
🗣 **[FILL IN: Sabatan mentors / coach name.]**

## Curveballs

### "Did you build this with AI? Do you understand your own code?"
💡 Honest yes; you designed the system and its rules.
🗣 "Yes, we use AI coding tools, like most startups now. What we designed is the architecture and the rules: how each business's data is separated, what the AI is allowed to do, and the tests every change has to pass. I can walk you through how one business is blocked from seeing another's data." (Then file 01, C1.)
⛔ Never "I just prompted it." Never pretend you typed every line.

### "This sounds too big for three students."
💡 Agree it's big, then show why it's doable.
🗣 "It is big, which is why we built one shared core. Books, BIR, schedule and the AI are built once, and each industry system is a smaller module on top. And it's already live at juanstack.com."

### "Can you show us it working?"
💡 Be ready before you go up.
🗣 Have **juanstack.com open on a phone, logged into a demo account with sample data.** Show one thing in under 30 seconds: ask Juan "What's due this month?" or open an invoice with its 2307 link.
If the internet fails: "It's live at juanstack.com; our slides show the real screens."

### "What's your biggest weakness?"
💡 Name a real one + the fix.
🗣 "We don't have paying users yet, and our tax rules aren't CPA-certified yet. Both are the first things this funding solves, and we won't charge for tax work until the CPA signs off."

### "Why should we pick you over the other teams?"
💡 Built, validated, sustainable.
🗣 "Three reasons. It's built, not just an idea: it's live. The problem is validated by real law offices and a contractor. And it pays for itself at just 15 users, so the grant grows it instead of keeping it alive."

### "What if you don't win?"
🗣 "We continue. Pilots through NVSU Sabatan start either way. The grant just lets us do security testing and CPA certification sooner."

### "Why 'JuanStack'?"
🗣 "'Juan' is every Filipino, Juan dela Cruz. 'Stack' is the full set of tools a business needs. Every Juan's AI business assistant."

### "Is your name or logo registered?"
🗣 "Not yet. DTI and IPOPHL registration are part of registering the business in months 1 to 3."

### "Is your app approved by BIR?"
🗣 "Not yet. Filing is turned off in our beta on purpose. Filing directly needs BIR certification as a Tax Software Provider, which is on our roadmap." (Details: file 02.)

### "What if a judge points out something wrong in your concept note?"
💡 Thank them, correct it plainly, move on.
🗣 "Thank you, you're right. [Correct fact]. We'll fix it in the next version."
Known gaps (file 08): hosting is **Vercel's free tier** today, Netlify or Vercel Pro at paid launch · receipt scanning **in development** · SLSP **next** · industry systems **being brought in**.

### "Your slides say X but you just said Y."
🗣 "Good catch. The correct figure is ___." Don't explain why it was wrong; just correct it.

### A judge is clearly unimpressed or harsh
💡 Stay warm. Don't defend; agree with the fair part.
🗣 "That's fair. Here's how we're addressing it…" One calm answer beats three defensive ones.
